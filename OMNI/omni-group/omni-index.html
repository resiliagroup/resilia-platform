Methodology

The HFE-360 Framework

Three dimensions, one resilience score. Developed with ITU AYM, validated across 600+ units in Turkey.

H
Hazard Assessment
Identification and scoring of external threats — seismic, cyber, flood, supply chain disruption and more.
F
Fragility Analysis
Internal vulnerability mapping — structural, operational, human and systemic weaknesses scored per unit.
E
Efficiency Capacity
Response and recovery capability — plans, resources, training and cross-unit coordination measured.

Platform

Four integrated modules

From initial risk scoring to real-time crisis response — the full BCM lifecycle in one platform.

HFE — RISK
Risk Scoring
Automated HFE-360 scoring engine with scenario-based assessment forms for every unit type.
  • Multi-scenario assessment
  • Automated score calculation
  • Regulatory threshold alerts
HFE — IMPACT
Impact Analysis
Business Impact Analysis with financial modeling, dependency mapping and recovery prioritization.
  • BIA questionnaires
  • Cost modeling engine
  • Critical path analysis
HFE — PLAN
Continuity Planning
Dynamic BCM plan generation with exercise scheduling, version control and audit trails.
  • Plan templates by sector
  • Exercise management
  • ISO 22301 alignment
